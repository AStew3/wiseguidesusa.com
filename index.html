How to create a guide

Whether it's onboarding, commissioning a site, or closing the books, the path is the same.

  1. Add the browser extension. Authors install it for Chrome or Edge in a minute. Learners need nothing.
  2. Capture the process. Record the task, import a document, or use live fill on a form.
  3. Polish the draft. Reword a step, add a tip or safety warning, mark up a picture.
  4. Review and publish. A reviewer approves it, and your team can use it right away.
  5. Share and assign. Send a link, export it, print a QR label, or assign it as a task.
  6. Track who's ready. See views, sign-offs and finished tasks in one place.

Wise Guides FAQ

What do my people need to install?

Nothing for learners: guides open in any browser, on a computer or phone. Authors add the Wise Guides browser extension for Chrome or Edge to record tasks and use Guide Me.

Where is our data kept?

In your own Wise Guides instance, on your servers or hosted for you, separate from every other customer. It's backed up daily, and you can copy the backups wherever you like.

Can we use the documents we already have?

Yes. Import Word, PDF, PowerPoint and Excel files or photos of printed procedures, and Wise Guides turns them into step-by-step guides with the pictures in the right places.

Does it work in Korean?

Yes. Guides can be read aloud in Korean, and Korean documents can be turned into English guides.

Can guides be used on the shop floor or in the field?

Yes. Guides open on phones, can be read aloud, and QR labels on equipment open the right procedure with one scan.

How do reviews and approvals work?

Authors send a draft for review; a reviewer approves it before anyone else sees it. Reviewers can't approve their own drafts, and every change is recorded.

How long does it take to get started?

You can record and publish your first guide on day one. We set up your instance and walk your team through it.

How does pricing work?

You pay per seat (each person who uses Wise Guides): Basic is $5.99, Team $9.99 and Business $14.99 per seat per month, and Enterprise is quoted. Annual billing saves 20%. There's no minimum number of seats, and you can cancel anytime.

What's free in the first month?

Web capture (recording tasks in your browser) is free for the first 30 days of a new workspace, so you can build your first guides before choosing a plan. Your guides stay available afterwards.
